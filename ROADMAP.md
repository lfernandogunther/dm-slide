# reel-queue — Roadmap

## 01 · MVP — the player (current)
A player over any Instagram DM conversation, 1:1 or group: every reel others sent, in the order
sent, one at a time. Walk with A/D, react ❤️ with F, reply under the video (quoting the reel or
not). Older reels load as you go back. Spec: `specs/01-mvp/`.

## 02 · Filters (future)
Pick which reels enter the player: only the ones without my reaction, only from one sender (in
groups), a date range. The first filter was always going to be "without my ❤️"; the MVP proved
the player first.

## 03 · Faster reactions & replies (future)
Other reactions besides ❤️ on single keys, and quick replies (a short list of canned messages on
number keys).

## 04 · Beyond reels (future)
Posts and photos shared in DMs go through the same player.

## 05 · Settings & polish (future)
An options page (defaults for auto-advance and quote, key remapping). Possibly a Chrome Web Store
release, if someone other than me wants it.
