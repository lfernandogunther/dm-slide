    Flow map

    1. S1 · Conversationreuses: Instagram Direct Instagram's own page, untouched. The extension only injects the "▶ Reel Queue" button in the thread header, with how many reels still lack your ❤️.
    2. ↓ ▶ Reel Queue → starts at the oldest reel without your ❤️
      ↓ click a reel in the chat → starts at that reel
      ↑ Esc / ✕ → back to the chat, ❤️ and replies already in it
    3. S2 · Playernew: Player Overlay on the thread. Every reel others sent, in the order sent. A / D (or ← / →) walk through them; F reacts ❤️ to the message; the reply box sits right under the video and goes to the chat. The strip on the left jumps anywhere and fetches older reels as you scroll up (lazy, one page at a time).

    Rules shown here

    • No filter: every reel, in the order sent. ❤️ only marks it (strip + counter).
    • Reels you sent are left out.
    • At the last reel, → stops; the counter says what's left without ❤️.
    • Groups work the same; the top of the video names the sender.

    Decide while clicking

    • Next after ❤️: on by default?
    • Quote this reel: on by default?
    • Entry point: header button, or the toolbar icon?
